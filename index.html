Values use market prices from Scryfall, which come mostly from TCGplayer. They assume near-mint English copies, so they don't change for condition or language. A store buying from you will usually pay less.

Back up your collection

Your collection lives only in this browser. Save a backup regularly, and use it to move your cards to another device.

Restore or import

Use a Longbox backup, or a CSV exported from an app or site. Cards are added to what's already here.

Prices

Prices update once a day when you open Longbox. Scryfall only changes them once a day, so updating more often won't show anything new.

Keep it on this device

On a phone, add Longbox to your home screen. Some phone browsers clear site data that hasn't been used for a while, and an installed app is safer.

Erase everything

Removes every card from this browser. This can't be undone unless you have a backup.
